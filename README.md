## 👨‍💻 About me

I turn complex requirements into **scalable, maintainable e-commerce and B2B platforms** aligned with the business. 7+ years building software for real businesses — from the initial analysis through implementation and long-term evolution.

- 🛒 **E-commerce platforms** — B2B & B2C, multi-organization, sales channels, price lists, stock and orders
- 🔌 **System integrations** — ERPs, payment gateways, logistics providers and external APIs (15+ in production)
- 🧱 **Architecture & APIs** — multi-tenant designs, data flows, performance and scalability
- 👥 **Technical leadership** — currently leading a team of 3 developers at [Minimalart](https://minimalart.co)
- 🤖 **AI-assisted development** — using AI to speed up analysis, testing and documentation without giving up control over quality

## 💡 How I build

Modularity is non-negotiable: small cohesive units, design by contract, dependency inversion, low coupling and no dependency cycles. Patterns only where they solve a concrete problem — **KISS over dogma**.
